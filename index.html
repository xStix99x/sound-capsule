Sound Capsule

Record a story, song, or lullaby for Alice directly from your phone! Feel free to include a personal note as well.

Please record in a quiet room, and be sure you aren't using any bluetooth headphones or hearing aids.

  1. Hold the phone about 6 to 8 inches away from your mouth.
  2. Turn off background TVs, fans, or AC units.
  3. Do not use bluetooth headphones or hearing aids; a dedicated microphone is okay.
